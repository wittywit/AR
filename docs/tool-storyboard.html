Storyboard

Four beats. Write them before you touch a tool.

Every AR piece that works is the same four beats. Most that fail are missing beat two. nobody told the viewer to raise their phone. Fill one sheet per artifact. Typing saves to this device as you go; nothing leaves the phone.

Living Lookbook Brand Filter Placed Product How to use this

Artifact one · The Living Lookbook

A printed page that plays. Image target → video or artwork.

Artifact two · The Brand Filter

A face effect someone would use twice. Face landmarks → brand object.

Artifact three · The Placed Product

One object, real size, in a real room. Plane → 3D model.

How to use this

Fifteen minutes per sheet. Each line is a decision that the build will be held to, so write the sentence you would use to describe the piece to a client rather than a description of a feature.

BeatThe failure it prevents
TriggerBuilding something with nothing to point at. Half of first-time AR projects never decide what the camera is actually looking for.
InvitationA perfect experience nobody opens. If there is no printed line, no QR, no sign and no sales assistant saying it, there is no audience.
PayloadShowing everything at once. One idea per trigger; a rotating logo restates the brand mark without adding an idea.
PayoffA moment with no exit. What does the viewer do next, and what do they carry away when the phone goes back in the pocket?

Before you leave the sheet

Read beat four aloud. If the stated payoff is only that the viewer finds it impressive, beat three needs rewriting.

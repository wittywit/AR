Trackability Scorer

Assess a page for trackability before committing it to print.

The camera does not see your artwork. It sees corners, points where the image changes sharply in two directions at once. This tool finds exactly those points, the same way a tracking library does, and tells you whether there are enough of them, spread widely enough, and different enough from each other.

How to fix a low score

The complaintThe fix, in design terms
Too few pointsYour artwork is too clean. Add photographic texture, hand type, fabric weave, a printed grain. Empty white space is invisible to a camera.
Points all clusteredEverything interesting sits in the middle. Push detail towards the edges, or the tracking dies the moment a thumb covers the centre.
Low contrastTonal, whisper-quiet palettes photograph beautifully and track terribly. Keep the mood; add one hard-edged element.
Low uniquenessA repeating check, stripe or monogram looks the same everywhere, so the camera cannot tell which part it is looking at. Break the repeat with one asymmetric element.
No texture, no tracking.

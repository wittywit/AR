Print → AR

The Living Lookbook. A printed page becomes a trigger, and something happens on top of it.

This is the whole of image-target AR, with nothing hidden: a trigger the camera recognises, a payload attached to it, and tracking that keeps the payload registered as you move. Nothing is uploaded. Everything runs in this browser.

1 · The trigger

Print this page, or open it on a second screen. A laptop or another phone works.

Open the practice target →

Using your own artwork? Score it in sim-tracking first, then compile it to a .mind file with the MindAR image compiler and load it below. A target that scores under 50 will waste your afternoon.

2 · The payload

Pick what appears on top of the page. A short video is the classic living-lookbook move; a still is fine.

Leave it empty and you get a built-in placeholder card, which is enough to prove the mechanic works before you have finished shooting.

3 · Run it

The camera needs HTTPS or localhost. If the button does nothing, you opened this file directly from disk. See the note at the bottom.

What to watch for

Do thisAnd notice
Tilt the page to forty-five degreesWhere it gives up. That is your real-world viewing angle budget.
Cover a third of the pageWhich third kills it. That is what a thumb, a fold or a staple does.
Walk away from itThe distance at which it drops. Print bigger and it holds longer.
Move it under a lamp, then a windowGlare erases features exactly like darkness does.
Time from opening to seeing somethingIf it is over eight seconds, nobody in a real shop will wait.

If it will not start

A browser will only hand over the camera on a secure origin. Serve this folder instead of double-clicking it:

cd docs
python3 -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000/ar-print.html. On a phone, reach your laptop's address over the same Wi-Fi, or put the folder on any HTTPS host.

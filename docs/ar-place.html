Place → AR

The Placed Product. A 3D object standing on a real floor, at its real size.

This page hands your model to the phone's own AR viewer: Scene Viewer on Android, AR Quick Look on iOS. No app, no account, no code. The web page is only the doorway.

1 · Load your own model

Nothing uploads. The file is read straight off this device. No .usdz? iPhones will still show the preview here, but the AR button needs one.

Showing the built-in demo model.

2 · Get the scale right

AR has one unit: the metre. 1 unit = 1 m. If your bag comes into the room the size of a sofa, the model was not authored in metres. It was authored in centimetres or inches and never converted.

At 1.00× the model reads as its authored size.

The slider is a rehearsal only. In real AR the object is placed at true size, because ar-scale="fixed" is set, which is what a product needs. Pinch-to-resize conceals an incorrectly authored model rather than correcting it.

3 · Believability switches

Pull the shadow to zero. The object stops sitting on the floor and starts floating in front of it. That single dark smudge under the object is doing more work than any amount of material detail. Match exposure to the room: an object lit brighter than its surroundings reads as a sticker.

Run it properly

StepWhat to look for
Point at the floorSweep slowly. A plain, evenly-lit floor with some texture finds a plane in a second or two. Polished marble and plain carpet are the hard cases.
Place itDoes it land at the size you expected? Note the answer before you adjust anything.
Walk a full circleThe object should stay put. If it slides with you, the anchor is weak. That is drift, and it is a lighting and texture problem, not a model problem.
Kneel downLook at where the object meets the floor. That contact edge is where believability is won or lost.
Time itFrom tapping the button to seeing the object: under eight seconds, or the viewer is gone.

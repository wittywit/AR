Handed out Day 1

The brief · XR Capsule

You are the communication team for a six-piece capsule collection. Use a brand from your portfolio or invent one. It needs a name, a point of view, and a customer you can describe in one sentence.

Over three days you produce three AR artifacts. Together they must read as one campaign, not three exercises.

ArtifactWhat it is
The Living LookbookOne printed page. Pointing a phone at it reveals the collection. The print must still work as print if nobody scans it.
The Brand FilterA face or accessory effect carrying your own textile print or brand mark. One interaction, not five.
The Placed ProductYour hero piece, scanned on a phone, placed at true scale in a real room.
PlusA 60-second case reel and a one-page campaign sheet.

Teams of 2–3. Everything shot, scanned and built on your own phones.

Non-negotiables

  • One visual system across all three artifacts. Type, colour and tone must match.
  • The printed page carries a legible instruction line. If a stranger cannot work out what to do in eight seconds, it has failed.
  • True scale on the placed product. A bag the size of a sofa fails that artifact outright.
  • Every AR piece tested on a phone that is not yours.

The question every team gets asked

If the phone was taken away, what is left?

If the answer is "nothing", the AR was decoration. If the answer is "a strong printed campaign that is extended by a phone", the objective of the module has been met.

Reference

How the 100 marks break down

CriterionMarksFull marks looks like
1Campaign coherence20A stranger seeing one artifact could guess the other two.
2The Living Lookbook15Tracks fast, holds steady, reveal motivated by the design. Print stands alone.
3The Brand Filter15Anchors cleanly, does not drift on a moving face, carries the brand without a logo on a forehead.
4The Placed Product15Clean scan within budget, true scale, shadow contact reads, survives being walked around.
5Craft of the AR itself10Loads fast, no drift, no jitter, sensible behaviour when tracking is lost.
6Vocabulary and reasoning10The team explains its choices using trigger, anchor, tracking, occlusion, drift and payload correctly.
7The reel and the sheet10Documents the experience itself rather than a screen recording of a menu.
8Process5Storyboard, target clinic before and after, asset-gate pass, evidence of testing on other people's phones.

Distinction requires full marks on criterion six. A student who builds a beautiful filter but cannot say why the anchor drifted has learned a template, not a medium.
